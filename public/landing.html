Your anime catalog, always updating

Browse the anime published by our editors, search your favorite series and discover newly synchronized episodes.

How it works

AniJs checks the published catalog every 30 seconds while this page is visible. New publications and synchronized episodes appear automatically. Playback availability depends on the servers returned by AniKotoAPI.

What is inside

Curated catalog — browse published titles managed by our editors, with automatic episode synchronization and connection status.

Subtitles you control — colour, opacity, size, typeface, edge, background and position, all remembered between visits.

Indonesian machine translation — for episodes whose source has no Indonesian track. Translations are cached, so each episode is only ever translated once.

Your own stream proxy — playlists and segments pass through your server against a destination allowlist, rather than being fetched straight from a third party.

Built to be read — genre and A-Z filters, paged listings, an episode sidebar, and a player that behaves on a phone as well as a desktop.

Powered by the AniKoto API. Source on GitHub.
